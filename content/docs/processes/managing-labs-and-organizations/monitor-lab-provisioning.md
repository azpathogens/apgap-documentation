# Monitor lab provisioning
> [!WARNING]
> **The Permissions required for this operation are Platform Admin**

To check provisioning status:
1. In the Labs list view, the right upper corner of a lab tile shows the status of the project.
2. The status can be:
    - **Pending**: the lab is in the process of being created.
    - **Active**: the lab has been successfully created.
    - **Failed**: something went wrong during provisioning and the lab could not be created. 
3. If lab creation failed, you can see an error log by clicking on "Build logs".
4. You can delete a failed lab by clicking on the trash can icon in the bottom-right corner of the lab tile.
