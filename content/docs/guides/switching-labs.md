# Switching Labs

Most of the operations in APGAP are scoped to a single lab. If you are a member of multiple labs, you can switch between them in the upper left corner of the UI.

Where you see the name of your current lab and a dropdown, click the dropdown to see a list of all the labs you belong to. You can also click **See All Labs** to see this same list wiht more details such as departnment, number of projects, and your role in each lab.

Simply click on the lab name to switch to it and you will be taken to the home page for that lab.
